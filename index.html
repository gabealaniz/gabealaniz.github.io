Hi! I'm Gabriel.

I'm a pre-doctoral researcher pursuing a Ph.D. in clinical psychology, advised by Dana Saifan, Ph.D. and Blanche Wright, Ph.D. I recently completed my M.A. in Psychology at California State University, Los Angeles, and I'm now a visiting scholar in the DIMENSION Lab at the University of Oregon.

I study the social determinants of mental health and how mental health services can better reach and serve the communities that need them. My interests include mental health services and policy research, implementation science, and intervention development. To do this work, I use mixed methods, advanced statistical approaches, and community-based participatory research.

Right now, that means projects on codeswitching and mental health among Muslim American young adults, family pressure around college and well-being among students of color, and community readiness for digital evidence-based practices with Latinx stakeholders.

I love mentoring undergraduate students, especially on preparing for research conferences, developing research skills, and planning next steps in academia. If you're a student with questions, please visit the Students page.
