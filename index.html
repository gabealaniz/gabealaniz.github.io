I am a psychology researcher with an M.A. in Psychology from California State University, Los Angeles, and a B.A. in Psychology from the University of California, Los Angeles.

My research interests center on mental health disparities, implementation science, and improving access to evidence-based mental health services.

I am particularly interested in understanding how organizational and systemic factors influence the adoption and implementation of evidence-based practices in schools and community settings, with an emphasis on Latine youth and families.
